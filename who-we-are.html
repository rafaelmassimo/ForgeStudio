Meet The Founder

Gabriela has been a Yoga Alliance Certified since 2020 and a teacher for many years throughout the Fraser Valley, becoming renown for her expertise, playfulness and ability to connect with her students.

She is very focused on building a unique approach to fitness and one that guides people into building a better life through strength.

She is very focused on building a unique approach to fitness and one that guides people into building not only a better body, but enjoying the journey of building a better life.

No Miracle diets. No Shortcuts. Gabi believes in a well balanced, disciplined routine that makes you live life on your own terms. All of that with a good dose of humor!

A journey through spirituality and
a better lifestyle

Gabriela’s teaching career starts with a search for purpose and a better lifestyle. She previously practiced Jiu Jitsu and Yoga, which although for a very brief moment, brought her immense joy and a sense of personal satisfaction.

Gabi traveled through Asia, including India and Cambodia, where she explored ancient yoga and meditation traditions and gained professional experience working at a retreat.

She later evolved that practice into a structured routine that she could follow and eventually transformed that into discipline and action that shifted her career into a different direction.

Forging the next chapter

That teaching eventually bloomed into resilience, diligence and a work ethic. Now, Gabriela applies those same values into her classes to provide her students with a mindset of constant growth and knowledge.

“More than just strength training, I’ll share my knowledge and passion so you can build a high-energy journey with a caring community.”

Now, Gabriela’s new chapter is building a wellness centre that starts with FORGE Studio as a pivotal point to a more ambitious dream for the Fraser Valley community.
