Less intimidation,
more confidence.

Our No Pressure Space provides you all the amenities that you need to go through the journey of a transformation in your physique and your wellbeing. No crowd, no sharing equipment, no uncomfortable looks. Just you, and your determination to become the best version of yourself.

You can choose between having Exclusive Sessions, with the whole boutique space rented for you; be a part of a Collective Session with up to four people to build strength together, or to have one of our professional Strength Trainers guide you towards the process from beggining to end.

A 3 month commitment.
A Lifetime change.

At FORGE, we aim to develop with your body and quality of life so you can deepen your connection with your body and feel amazing with consistency.

To achieve such lasting benefits, accountability is equally as important in the process. You have to be willing to show up consistently and build with us the results you want to have.

And to achieve your dream body, you also need a dream journey. To be able to enjoy the process, to be in an environment that feels comfortable, inspiring and incentivize you to be the best version of yourself.

Our 3 month program is build exactly to provide you a timeline to start that pain-free journey that you have been looking for. You get to become proud of yourself and your dedication, in your own pace, with no pressure.
